## 4. Game Formats to Explore for a Daily Games Platform

| Priority | Format | Examples | Design Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fandom attribute deduction | LoLdle, Squirdle | Reusable game engine across multiple franchises. |
| 2 | Category intersection grids | PokeDoku, GeoGrid | Reward rare, knowledge-intensive answers. |
| 3 | Map precision challenges | HexHunt | Interactive, score-driven geography experience. |
| 4 | Shape-fitting puzzles | Patches, Flipart | Visual gameplay with limited text. |
| 5 | Constraint-based placement | Pips | Distinct from standard Sudoku. |
| 6 | Progressive image reveals | GuessThe.Game, Gamedle | Straightforward daily reveal loop. |
| 7 | Estimation challenges | HowLongToBeat | Adaptable across many themes. |
| 8 | Fandom grouping puzzles | Pokennection, CraftConnections | Connections-style grouping with franchise content. |
| 9 | Chess or card strategy variations | Really Bad Chess, Pile-Up Poker | Strategic gameplay rather than trivia. |
| 10 | Compound-word construction | Circuits | Different from standard word guessing. |

> **Note:** Independent fandom games may use copyrighted characters, images, sounds, and brands. Linking to them is different from reproducing their assets or integrating their gameplay. Verify websites, permissions, and availability before publishing or integrating them.
